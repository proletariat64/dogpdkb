OCR reuse evidence (mdocr-ocr-2)
================================

Only v2 `mdocr run` evidence is eligible for reuse. `mdcompare compare` stays
offline and never runs OCR. Both sides must declare compatible configuration
and provide original-image and OCR-block paths and SHA-256 digests. Each image
record must bind a declared original resource; its OCR block must have a v2
header, matching engine and original SHA-256, a nonempty body, and an end
marker. LLM reuse additionally requires matching context SHA-256.

Missing or incompatible evidence cannot authorize a skip. OCR block bytes
are not authored Markdown; use `mdcompare semantic` for authored-body routing.

# Apply manifest and recovery contract

Choose exactly one of --input DIFFERENCES and --resume RUN. Input is the frozen
dogpdkb-differences-1 JSON emitted by discover, not Markdown. Use discover's saved
artifact rather than assembling production selections manually. Its fields are
schema, book, run_id, captured_at, listing, documents, absent, ignored and cutoff;
selected document fields include document_id, observed version, title, remote
path, source_url, format, metadata, reasons and prior local facts. Run/book and
frozen targets must agree. Read discover --help --full for the complete listing
and difference field contract.

apply exports, catalogs, compares, freezes a plan, performs best-effort OCR and
publishes complete usable corpus Markdown. It does not compile the graph.
--stop-after plan saves all raw/comparison inputs and exits 0 before OCR or
corpus publication. Resume checks saved inputs/witnesses and cannot repair
deleted/changed plan inputs by downloading them again. A source change or
intervening incompatible publication requires a fresh discovery and plan.

The dogpdkb-compile-1 JSON contains schema, book (key), run_id, documents and
failures. Minimal shape (paths and witnesses must actually be produced by apply):

```json
{"schema":"dogpdkb-compile-1","book":"funds","run_id":"run-example","documents":[{"document_id":"42","version":{"value":"2026-11-13T00:00:00Z","precision":"instant","utc":"2026-11-13T00:00:00+00:00"},"path":"/absolute/corpus/source.md","input_copy":"/absolute/state/funds/inputs/42/source.md","metadata":{"name":"Example","logical_path":"folder","source_ref":"https://example.invalid/source"},"action":"compile","reason_codes":["BODY_CHANGED"]}],"failures":[]}
```

Document actions: compile (complete current source), metadata (source metadata
maintenance only), reuse (no graph content work). Paths identify full published
Markdown; input_copy is its exact durable witness. baseline_version identifies
the body comparison baseline. previous_graph freezes prior graph facts when
known; optional prior facts may be absent. Version is observed, never synthesized.
Failures carry step/document/version/reason and evidence when available; they
remain separate from usable documents and can cause exit 2.

stdout fields are exit_code, paused, manifest (path or null), run (run.json
path). stderr carries live diagnostics. OCR partial can still publish usable
bodies; source/graph facts advance only after their actual success. Same-version
failed/unknown steps stay suppressed. A repeated invocation may perform downloads
or model calls only for eligible work; do not blindly repeat to fix a failed step.
